Make every photo square for HomeView

Go through your photos one at a time and choose how each one becomes square. Every photo is saved to the output folder as soon as you decide, and your progress is saved there too, so you can stop and resume any time.

  1. Open your photos folder. Subfolders are included, and the output keeps the same folder structure.
  2. Choose an empty output folder. Your originals are never modified.
  3. For each photo, choose Crop, Solid (white), Blur fill, or Mirror, then press Enter.

You can also drag a folder onto this window.

Match your screen to real size

Hold a standard bank or credit card flat against your screen, then drag the slider until the outline is exactly the size of the card. You only need to do this once per monitor.

Card
☾

Editing in the evening? Check Night Shift. It tints your screen orange, so colors look warmer than they really are, and warmth or filter choices made now can come out too cool. Turn it off before judging color: Control Center → Display → Night Shift. True Tone does the same thing more subtly.

Browsers can’t detect Night Shift, so this is a reminder based on the time of day, not a detection.
